# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing codebase: Vite + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui, pre-rendered with vite-react-ssg, deployed on Vercel. Reservation emails currently sent with EmailJS from the browser (confirmed by the user to stay for now).

## Users

Primary: Italian-speaking families and private hosts near Rome planning a celebration (communions, christenings, confirmations, birthdays, graduations), arriving from search or the home page. They want to know quickly if the venue fits their guest count, date and rules, then send a request. Secondary (not the lead): companies and film/photo productions.

## Product Purpose

Green Woody Home is a private eco-friendly villa in Castel di Decima (Rome) with a 1,500 m² garden, rented for stays and also as a venue for parties and events. The events page exists to turn "can I hold my event there?" into a quote request. Success: visitors understand capacity and rules and submit the shared reservation form.

## Positioning

A private villa with its own garden, solar-powered, 30 minutes from central Rome and 15 from the sea, where the whole property is yours for the event and you can also sleep there.

## Operating Context

Visitors request availability through the shared calendar and reservation form; the owners reply with a direct proposal. The calendar and form appear together on every page that takes bookings. Owners handle replies personally; no online payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Events up to 100 guests; overnight stay possible for up to 6 people.
- All event types accepted (graduations, birthdays, corporate events, photo shoots, christenings, confirmations, communions, film productions, and more).
- Music and noise allowed until 00:00.
- Catering, rentals and event set-ups are NOT included.
- Garden 1,500 m², barbecue, free parking, EV charger, kitchen, Wi-Fi, air conditioning.
- Languages: English and Italian, switched client-side (no separate URLs yet).
- The events page must always show the availability calendar together with the reservation form. The form gains a guest-count field capped at 100 and an event-type field; both go into the email body so the email structure does not change.
- Undecided: prices (on request), whether local partners (chef, tennis, gladiator experience) serve events.

## Brand Commitments

Existing logo (public/logo.png), mint green and warm sand palette, Playfair Display headings and Inter body already shipped on the site. Real photos live in public/photos.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos of the villa and garden (public/photos). No testimonials, event photos, prices or client names exist; do not fabricate them. AI-generated event imagery, if used, is illustrative and must be labelled as such, since setups are not included.

## Product Principles

1. Answer the host's practical questions (how many, until when, what is included) before selling.
2. Never imply services that are not included; setups, catering and rentals are the client's.
3. One path to action: calendar and form together, always visible on the page.
4. Warm and personal, a family-run villa rather than an event agency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Italian and English parity; readable on phones where most families will browse.
